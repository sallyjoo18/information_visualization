Team 15 · Global Health · 1980–2024

From Global Progress to Uneven Recovery

Childhood vaccination is one of public health's great success stories — but the children still being missed cluster in the same fragile places. COVID-19 showed how quickly four decades of gains can slip.

Communication Goals

  1. Trace the global rise: Show how routine childhood vaccination climbed from near zero to near universal — starting with polio and following three diseases with consistent colors throughout.
  2. Highlight geographic inequality: Reveal where gaps cluster in 2024 via stacked maps that reuse each disease's color ramp from Chart 1.
  3. Explore structural drivers: Investigate whether wealth and development explain who vaccinates well — and who falls behind despite resources.
  4. Examine COVID disruption: Show a before/during/after view of whether progress held or slipped — closing the story on the same three diseases.
